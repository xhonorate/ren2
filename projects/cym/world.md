# World

## Setting

A small town built around its college, autumn into winter. The hospital sits on the edge of town; the college's old teaching block on the rise above the main street; between them a three-storey apartment building with thin walls, a landlady's office on the ground floor, and a river path behind it that he walks when he cannot sleep. The main street has a diner, a laundrette, and not much else open after nine.
