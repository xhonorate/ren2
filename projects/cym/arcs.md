# Arcs

## Overall

**Shape: hub calendar** (DESIGN.md §8). Three acts over one autumn term.

- **Act 1 — common (8 scenes, the first two weeks).** He wakes; Natalie,
  Diana and Claire in the hospital; move-in day with Bella and Alice; first
  day at college with Yuki and Victoria; Natalie's first home visit; the
  diner breakfast where Diana lays out the investigation; the end of the
  second week, when the building and the college have become a shape he can
  walk through. Every woman has had one scene that is hers. The last common
  scene ends on the first free evening.
- **Act 2 — the calendar (10 hub weeks).** Each hub is a short common scene
  that carries the season (week 3 the first cold night, week 4 Halloween,
  week 6 the river fog, week 7 first snow, week 9 exams, week 10 the last
  day of term) and ends on the choice of whose evening it is. Each option
  calls the next spoke of that route; spokes return to the hub. Any route
  can be advanced in any week; nothing closes another until the lock. A
  route has 5 spokes, so in 10 weeks the player can finish two routes, or
  sample five. That is the replay.
- **Act 3 — the lock and the ending (1 + 7 scenes).** The last day of term:
  winter break, and the question of where he spends it. Every route whose
  5 spokes are done is offered. One is chosen; its ending scene plays, with
  a good or normal variant on affection. If none is finished: the alone
  ending, which is kind (he is better than he was; spring exists).

**The spine under the routes.** Three questions, answered in pieces, never
all on one route: *what happened that night*, *who his mother is*, and
*why each of these women has decided on him*. The third is the real plot.

### The night (bible truth; the game states only pieces of it)
Two weeks before the story, clearing his father's study, he found a box of
letters from his mother: one every birthday since he was three, addressed
to him, opened, kept from him. His step-mother had intercepted them. The
night of the "accident" he confronted her, packed a bag to leave, and on
the stairs she grabbed for the bag and he fell. She waited hours before
calling it in as a fall; that delay is what the hospital flagged. He
remembers the letters and the bag. He does not remember the stairs. The
letters are in his belongings, which Diana holds. The investigation is
open; the step-mother is barred from contact; she does not accept that.

### His mother (bible truth)
Alive, two towns over, a music teacher. She left when he was three, during
a long illness she has since recovered from; his father remarried; the
step-mother made sure she stayed gone. She has written every year. She was
at college with Victoria.

**Affection.** Every spoke has one or two choices worth +1/+2; endings are
good at `affection.<id> >= 6`, else normal. Route completion is
`flags.<id>_step == 5`; the hub options call `<id>_s{step+1}`.

**Explicit scenes.** Each route's spoke 4 is the first explicit scene and
the ending is the second; spokes 3 and 5 are suggestive. The mommy-domme
register is hers to offer in each route and his to accept; the spoke-3
choice is where he does.

## diana

*Premise:* the woman whose job is to make sure he is looked after decides,
one overstep at a time, that the person looking after him is her.
*What only her route learns:* the step-mother's prior complaint (his
father's, withdrawn), and the letters.

1. **Groceries.** She arrives with bags and cooks in his kitchen; "my boy"
   slips out and she pretends it didn't. Choice: let her stay for dinner or
   send her home with the leftovers.
2. **The file.** Her office. She tells him more than she should: there was
   a complaint, years ago, from his father; it was withdrawn. She gives him
   her personal number again, as if the first time didn't count.
3. **The dress.** She takes him to dinner "to celebrate the lease" in the
   one dress she owns. She names the line she has crossed. He answers.
4. **(explicit) Her place.** The first night; the maternal register begins
   because he asks for it, and she has been waiting to be asked.
5. **The door.** The step-mother comes to the building. Diana is there
   before anyone has called her; she stands in the doorway with the flat
   voice and the woman leaves. Upstairs, she shakes, and he holds her for
   the first time instead of the other way round. She gives him the box of
   letters from evidence.

*Ending — winter break at Diana's.* Good: "my boy", said in daylight, and
he says the word back. Normal: she keeps the file open and the key on the
hook, and tells him to take his time.

## claire

*Premise:* the therapist assigned to his trauma recovers the night with him
and loses, on purpose, the distance that let her do it.
*What only her route learns:* the memory itself, and the mother's letter.

1. **The hour.** Sessions; the tissue box he refuses; she extends the hour
   and bills for fifty minutes.
2. **The laundrette.** They meet off-hours by accident; she is in the
   casual goth, not the dress, and talks about herself for the first time:
   the bullying, the long grey year, why psychology.
3. **The letters.** Memory work in her office. The box comes back, the bag,
   his step-mother's hand. Not the stairs. She holds him. Afterwards she
   says, flat and precise, that she should not have, and that she will
   again.
4. **(explicit) Her place.** "Let it out" is hers; the soothing register
   is what she has always wanted to give someone and never been allowed to.
5. **Halloween.** The witch costume; the stairs come back on a walk by the
   river; she decides, that night, to stop being his therapist so she can
   be this, and refers him on. The cost is real and she pays it.

*Ending — winter break.* Good: she helps him write to his mother, and the
reply comes before the new year; he reads it with his head in her lap.
Normal: the letter is written and not yet sent; she tells him there is no
deadline on it.

## natalie

*Premise:* the nurse with eleven minutes gives him twelve, then an evening,
then the one thing she wants, which is something easy.
*What only her route learns:* she is the one who called it in; the injuries
were hours old when he arrived.

1. **Twelve minutes.** Home visit; she clocks the not-sleeping as a symptom
   and prescribes walking. Choice: argue, or walk.
2. **The river.** She walks with him after a shift, in the casual clothes,
   and tells him what she flagged and why. "Hours. Not minutes."
3. **Insomnia.** She treats it her way: procedural, matter-of-fact, and he
   discovers that being handled is restful. The choice is whether he says
   so.
4. **(explicit) After nights.** Her place at seven in the morning. Simple.
   "Mommy" is a tease that lands harder than she expected.
5. **Discharge.** The last official visit; the clipboard signed off. She has
   to decide whether to keep coming with no reason. She comes back the next
   day without the clipboard.

*Ending — a day off.* Good: the diner at six a.m., her sleeping on his
shoulder in the booth, the first time anyone has seen her off duty. Normal:
she keeps the Tuesday, and calls it a check-up.

## alice

*Premise:* the neighbour who wanted a child and got a wall between her and
one, and the question of what he lets her be.
*What only her route learns:* the photograph.

1. **Soup.** Dinner through the wall becomes dinner at hers. "Knock any
   time." Choice: knock that night, or not.
2. **The boxes.** She cleans his place (she does the whole building) and
   finds the photograph in a box: his mother, holding him. She cries. He
   doesn't, and she holds him anyway.
3. **The wall.** Pajamas, midnight, he knocks. Cuddling on the sofa with too
   many cushions; "you can call me whatever you like, honey." He tries the
   word.
4. **(explicit) Hers.** Nursing, spoiling, his pleasure first; the baby talk
   is sincere and he stops being embarrassed by it halfway through.
5. **Move in.** She asks. Her fears come out in order: old, unattractive,
   left. He answers each one; the choice is which he answers first.

*Ending — winter break.* Good: the boxes come through the wall; 2A is
empty; she says "Mommy's home" with the shopping and means every word.
Normal: he keeps 2A, the wall is thin, and she leaves a plate outside his
door every night anyway.

## bella

*Premise:* the landlady who despises her tenants goes upstairs to check on
one, and finds a boy who likes being told what to do.
*What only her route learns:* that she would throw anyone out for him.

1. **The first.** Rent day. She brings a space heater "so you stop running
   up my bill" and stays longer than a landlady should.
2. **Six a.m.** She orders him to the gym. He goes. The teasing starts and
   he does not mind it, which she notices.
3. **The books.** Her office, evening; the building is losing money; he
   helps and she hates that he can. The first order he obeys that has
   nothing to do with rent, and he chooses it out loud.
4. **(explicit) Her terms.** The degradation is offered as a question and
   taken as an answer; it is hers alone, and the narration never joins in.
5. **My building.** The step-mother at the front door. Bella throws her out
   with four words and the master keys in her fist. Upstairs, the softest
   she has ever been, for about a minute.

*Ending — winter break.* Good: she stops calling him kid and does not
notice she has. Normal: rent is waived for December and she dares him to
mention it.

## yuki

*Premise:* the classmate who has been through the same system finds, in
him, the first person she can say things to; the gap between what she can
say and what she has thought is the whole route.
*What only her route learns:* what Diana did for her, which tells him what
Diana is doing for him.

1. **The window seat.** The library; studying; the stutter on the first
   word of everything that matters.
2. **The dress form.** Her apartment, the cosplay half-made, the bunny suit
   under a sheet. She tells him about aging out, and that Diana still
   checks on her, and that Diana does that for no one else. "Except you."
3. **The long way.** The river path; she says the thing in a rush and
   hides in her cardigan. The choice is whether he lets her hide.
4. **(explicit) Hers.** Inexperienced dominance that needs reassurance at
   every step; he gives it; "mommy" is her idea and she apologises for it
   before he can say yes.
5. **The seminar.** She presents, because he is in the room. Victoria
   watches them both and says one dry thing.

*Ending — the convention.* Good: the bunny suit finished and worn in a
hall where nobody knows her, and one person who does. Normal: she practises
the second sentence and gets it out on the platform in January.

## victoria

*Premise:* the professor who decided on him before she knew why, and the
reason, which is his mother.
*What only her route learns:* who his mother is, and where.

1. **The footnotes.** She keeps him after the seminar; a reading list; a
   seat assigned.
2. **The register.** She recognises his surname and says nothing. The
   control tightens: his afternoons, his essays kept in a drawer.
3. **The photograph.** Dinner at hers, the evening clothes. She tells him
   she knew his mother at college, and shows him a photograph he has never
   seen. The choice is whether he asks the next question.
4. **(explicit) Owned.** Everything is hers and she says so; the yandere
   seam shows as devotion with teeth.
5. **Arranged.** She has found his mother and arranged a meeting without
   asking him, because that is what she does. He has to decide whether to
   go, and whether to forgive the arranging.

*Ending — the meeting.* Good: his mother, in person, in the diner, with
Victoria waiting in the car and not coming in until she is asked. Normal:
he does not go yet; Victoria's grip cracks, and what is underneath it is
someone who can wait.

## Rejected
- Exclusive routes with a single early lock: simpler to write, but it
  makes "whose evening" a one-time choice instead of the game.
- A separate "Fund"/benefactor mystery from the earlier premise draft:
  dropped; the step-mother and the letters carry the plot.
