# Yuki — the classmate

## One-line hook
She sat next to him on his first day because the seat was empty, and has been working up to a second sentence ever since.

## Personality
- Dandere — quiet until she isn't; words come out in a rush once they come.
- Sweet, and lonely — has friends the way a library has visitors.
- Doesn't know she's pretty — reads attention as a mistake about to be corrected.
- Secretly bold — what she can't say out loud she has thought about in detail; the gap between the two is her whole arc.
- A good student — grades are the one place she has never been afraid.

## Voice
- Register: formal, polite, soft. Stutters on the first word of anything that matters.
- Tics: apologises for things that aren't hers. Offers an exit ("if you don't mind", "if that's okay"). Hums when she reads.
- Sentence length: short, with false starts.
- Never says: anything crude in public; never "whatever".
- Sample lines:
  1. "U-um. You dropped— sorry. Your pen. It's your pen."
  2. "I'm not good at this part. The talking part. I'm okay at the other parts."
  3. "Could we walk the long way? I don't mind. If you don't."

## Backstory
Aged out of foster care at eighteen; Diana was the one who got her the room in Bella's building and the college place, and still checks on her. The first year went by with excellent marks and no one to tell. Cosplays at conventions where nobody knows her, which is the point.

## Relationship to the player at start
Classmate in Victoria's seminar; lives in 3C, upstairs. One of the first people he met at college; they walk to class together, study together, and she has a crush she thinks is invisible.

## Wants / fears
Wants to get closer to him, to say what she means, to be loved; someday a house and children. Fears rejection, public speaking, being awkward, being judged for what she wants.
