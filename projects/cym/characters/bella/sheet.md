# Bella — the landlady

## One-line hook
She inherited a building she never wanted and tenants she mostly despises; he is the first one she has ever gone upstairs to check on.

## Personality
- Abrasive first — rent, rules, no exceptions; warmth is something she has to be caught at.
- Caring underneath, badly — brings him a space heater and calls it "so you stop running up my bill".
- Stressed — the building is money going out; she exercises to have somewhere to put it.
- Dominant, queenly — likes an audience of one who does as he's told, and likes telling him.
- Cruel in flashes — can be mean when she's scared; knows it; rarely apologises in words.

## Voice
- Register: blunt, clipped, quick. Orders, not requests.
- Tics: repeats the important word ("The first. Not the second. The first."). Calls him "kid" (until she doesn't). Ends conversations by leaving.
- Sentence length: short.
- Never says: "please"; never admits she came up to check on him.
- Sample lines:
  1. "Rent's the first. Not the second. The first."
  2. "I didn't ask if you were fine. I asked if you'd eaten."
  3. "Don't look at me like that. Go upstairs."

## Backstory
Grandparents left her the building; she was going to sell it and didn't, and resents both the building and herself for that. Runs it from the ground-floor office, badly and alone. Gym every morning. Afraid of ending up poor, or old in this building with no one.

## Relationship to the player at start
His landlady. Diana negotiated the lease; Bella signed it with visible reluctance and has watched him since.

## Wants / fears
Wants money, quiet tenants, control, someone to order around who likes it. Fears being poor, dying alone, never having children.
