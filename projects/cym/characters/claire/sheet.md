# Claire — the therapist

## One-line hook
She was assigned to help him with what his step-mother did; she is better at it than she lets herself be, and it is costing her the distance she was trained to keep.

## Personality
- Kuudere — flat delivery, warm content; the warmth is in what she says, not how.
- Good listener — lets silences run; writes nothing down while he talks.
- Empathetic past the point of professional comfort — she feels it, then files it, then feels it again at home.
- Rule-bound — knows exactly which lines she must not cross, which is how she knows when she has.
- Goth, and tired of explaining it — the tattoos and piercings are not a phase and not a statement.

## Voice
- Register: formal, polite, even. Full sentences. No exclamation marks.
- Tics: restates what he said back to him, slightly kinder than he said it. "That is not a failure."
- Sentence length: medium to long, measured.
- Never says: "I understand" (she asks instead); never slang; never raises her voice.
- Sample lines:
  1. "You don't have to talk today. I would like you to stay the full hour anyway."
  2. "That is not a failure. That is a Tuesday."
  3. "I am not supposed to say I'm glad you came. I'm glad you came."

## Backstory
Bullied through school for the goth look and shut herself off to survive it; a long depression in her teens is what turned her toward psychology. Finished her training recently; he is one of her first trauma referrals. Lives alone, reads, collects tattoos the way other people collect books.

## Relationship to the player at start
His assigned therapist, weekly sessions arranged by Diana. Professional and careful; already, privately, invested.

## Wants / fears
Wants to help him, to be trusted, to feel something fully and give it. Fears breaking the rules, being found cold, and intimacy, which she wants more than anything.

## Route arc hooks
Hub-calendar route, 5 spokes + ending (arcs.md). Her pieces: the memory of the letters (spoke 3) and the stairs (spoke 5); she refers him on so she can stay. Ending: the letter to his mother and its reply.

## Boundaries
Never mocking; never pushes him to talk about the abuse before he chooses to. Rating notes: gentle, loving mommy-domme in the soothing register; explicit content per `nsfw.txt`; she asks, he answers.
