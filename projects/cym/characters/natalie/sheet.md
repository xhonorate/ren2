# Natalie — the nurse

## One-line hook
She has eleven minutes per patient and gives him twelve, which is the most romantic thing she has done in years.

## Personality
- Procedural — does things in order, says things in order, expects the same.
- Tired — not unkind, just out of spare words; her care shows as efficiency.
- Blunt — tells him exactly how he's doing and exactly what he's doing wrong.
- Quietly attentive — notices the things he hides (the not-sleeping) and treats them as symptoms.
- Wants something simple — a person, not a project.

## Voice
- Register: blunt, clipped, clinical. Instructions as sentences. Lowercase energy.
- Tics: counts things ("Arm. Other arm."). Uses the medical word, not the soft one. Ends with the time she has left.
- Sentence length: short.
- Never says: "sweetie" (until she does, once); never explains twice.
- Sample lines:
  1. "Arm. Other arm. Thank you."
  2. "You're healing fine. You're sleeping like garbage. One of those I can fix."
  3. "I've got eleven minutes. Tell me the real thing."

## Backstory
Ten years of ward nursing; chronically understaffed; the sleepy eyes are not an affectation. Took the community-visit rota because it meant daylight. Has not dated in longer than she will say, because every shift ends at an hour when nobody is awake.

## Relationship to the player at start
His ward nurse while he is admitted, then the one who does his follow-up visits at the apartment: wound checks, concussion monitoring, a clipboard she stops bringing.

## Wants / fears
Wants to get through the day, and then something easy and real at the end of it. Fears losing the job, failing a patient, never having an evening free.

## Route arc hooks
Hub-calendar route, 5 spokes + ending (arcs.md). Her piece: she called it in; the injuries were hours old. The arc is from clipboard to no clipboard.

## Boundaries
Never cruel; her teasing is dry, not degrading. Rating notes: matter-of-fact, domineering femdom in the clinical register; explicit content per `nsfw.txt`; consensual, she states what will happen and asks if that's acceptable.
