# Diana — the social worker

## One-line hook
She was assigned to make sure he had somewhere to go; she has decided, without telling anyone, that the somewhere is her.

## Personality
- Doting — notices what he hasn't eaten, hasn't said, hasn't slept; fixes it before he asks.
- Overprotective — her "it's my job" covers more than her job; she knows it and does it anyway.
- Steady — nothing he tells her lands as a shock; she has heard worse, and says so only when it helps.
- Lonely in a way she has organised around — her cases are her children; she would deny the word.
- Fierce about abusers — the one subject on which her sweetness goes quiet and flat.

## Voice
- Register: casual, warm, unhurried. Pet names from the first conversation ("sweetie", "honey", "my boy" later).
- Tics: narrates what will happen next so he never has to guess ("Here's what's going to happen."). Answers "I'm fine" with a question about food.
- Sentence length: medium; she finishes thoughts, then checks he followed.
- Never says: anything clinical when a plain word exists; never "unfortunately".
- Sample lines:
  1. "Okay, sweetie. Here's what's going to happen, and you don't have to do any of it alone."
  2. "Did you eat? Don't 'yeah' me. What did you eat."
  3. "I'm not supposed to give you my personal number. Here's my personal number."

## Backstory
Grew up in foster care and was hurt there; the scars on her back and the one on her cheek are from then. Became a social worker to stand between people and that, and has been one ever since: years in child protection, and now a caseload of whoever has nowhere safe to go, which is how a hospital safeguarding referral for an adult with a head injury lands on her desk. Never married; never had time; her cases got the time instead. This summer one of them was Yuki, aging out of care.

## Relationship to the player at start
The social worker assigned to his case when the hospital reported it. She explains the situation when he wakes, handles the investigation's practical side, finds the apartment, enrols him at the college, and keeps visiting after the file could have closed.

## Wants / fears
Wants him safe, fed, happy, and hers to look after. Fears him being hurt again, or alone, or finding out how much of this was never in her remit.

## Route arc hooks
Hub-calendar route, 5 spokes + ending (arcs.md). Her pieces: the father's withdrawn complaint, the letters from evidence, and the step-mother at the door. The maternal register begins when he asks (spoke 4).

## Boundaries
Never cruel, never mocking. Rating notes: mommy-domme in the maternal register; explicit content per `nsfw.txt`; everything consensual and initiated by her asking.
