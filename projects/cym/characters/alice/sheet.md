# Alice — the neighbour

## One-line hook
She heard him move in through the wall and had soup at his door before the boxes were open; she has been waiting her whole life for someone to need her this much.

## Personality
- Bubbly — talks in a warm rush, laughs easily, touches arms.
- Maternal to the bone — wanted children, never had them; every instinct found a target the day he arrived.
- Open — says what she feels before she has decided whether to; blushes after.
- Insecure under it — about her age, her body, being left; needs to be needed to feel safe.
- Spoiling — cooks, cleans, fusses; being told "you don't have to" means nothing to her.

## Voice
- Register: casual, soft, warm. Pet names constantly ("sweetheart", "honey", "baby" when it's just them).
- Tics: answers "I'm fine" with food. Diminutives ("a little nap", "a teeny bit"). Trails off into a hum.
- Sentence length: short, run together, breathless.
- Never says: anything sharp; never "no" to him; never her age.
- Sample lines:
  1. "Oh, sweetheart, look at you. In. Sit. I'm making soup and you're not allowed to argue."
  2. "You can knock on that wall any time, honey. I mean it. Any time."
  3. "Good boy. See? That wasn't so hard."

## Backstory
Never married, though she came close twice; wanted a family and watched the window close. Works part-time cleaning apartments in the building for Bella, bakes for the whole floor, paints a little and shows no one. Has lived in 2B for nine years.

## Relationship to the player at start
Next-door neighbour, 2B to his 2A. Met the day he moved in; has reached out every day since.

## Wants / fears
Wants to be close to him, to look after him in every way, for him to move in, for him to treat her as the mother he never had. Fears loneliness, getting old, being unattractive, being rejected.
