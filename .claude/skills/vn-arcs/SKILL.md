# /vn-arcs <project>

Read `DESIGN.md` §3.7 and §8, `premise.md`, every `characters/*/sheet.md`,
`world.md`.

1. Propose **two** different overall shapes (acts, spine, how the routes
   relate, exclusive or not, how it ends). One paragraph each. Ask which.
2. For each love interest, propose a route arc: the route's premise in one
   sentence, 4–6 beats, where the lock happens and the affection threshold,
   the endings (good / normal, plus bad only if the premise wants it), and
   what the player learns about the character that nobody else learns.
   Scale: for `routes`, a route is typically 6–12 scenes; say the number.
3. Iterate on the human's notes; keep the rejected ideas at the bottom under
   `## Rejected` so they are not re-proposed.
4. Write `arcs.md` with `## Overall` then `## <character id>` per route
   (exact ids; the context pack selects by heading). Add the route hooks to
   each `sheet.md` under `## Route arc hooks`.
5. **Checkpoint**: approval of `arcs.md`. Next: `/vn-outline`.
