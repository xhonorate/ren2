# Lantern Street

## Logline

A transfer student with one semester left finds the night market no one else seems to notice, and the two people who keep it running.

## Genre & tone

Slice-of-life romance with a thread of the uncanny. Warm, a little wistful, funny in the way friends are funny.

## Audience

Adults who grew up on 2000s VNs and want something short and sincere.

## Content rating

`suggestive`

## Branching level

`routes`

## The player

Kai, 21, a transfer student. Dry, observant, asks one question too many. Speaks in short sentences.

## Setting

A hill town in late autumn; a covered market street that is only open after dark.

## What makes this one different

The market is the third character. Every route changes what the market is.

## Things to avoid

No amnesia. No tragic dead parents. No villain.
