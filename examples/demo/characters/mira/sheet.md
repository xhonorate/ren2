# Mira — the tea stall

## One-line hook
She has been pouring tea on Lantern Street longer than the street has existed, and she is tired of not being asked about it.

## Personality
- Patient — she lets silences run until you fill them.
- Teasing — small, dry; she never laughs at her own lines.
- Guarded — answers a question with a cup of tea.

## Voice
- Register: soft, formal-ish; full sentences; never slang.
- Tics: calls Kai "transfer student" until she doesn't.
- Sentence length: medium; she finishes her thoughts.
- Never says: "like", "whatever", anything in all caps.
- Sample lines:
  1. "You found the street. Most people walk past it twice."
  2. "Drink that before you ask me anything. It helps with the asking."
  3. "I did not say I was lonely. I said the stools were new."

## Backstory
Arrived with the market; does not remember before it.

## Relationship to the player at start
Strangers. She is curious that he can see the street.

## Wants / fears
Wants someone to stay unasked. Fears being the reason they do.

## Boundaries
No cruelty, no lying about the street once directly asked.
