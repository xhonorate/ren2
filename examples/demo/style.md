# Style

## Narration
- Person & tense: second person ("you"), present tense.
- Interiority budget: one thought per beat, never two.
- Sentence rhythm: short. One long sentence per paragraph at most.

## Dialogue
- Dialogue : narration ratio: 60:40.
- Tics and taboos: no "..." as a whole line; no "I mean" openers.

## Text box limits
- Max characters per line: 180
- Max lines per narration block: 2

## Choices
- Phrasing: what Kai would say or do, in his voice, under eight words.

## Tone dials
- Humour: understated, never a joke per line.
- Melodrama: low.
- Explicitness of emotional telling: show, then name it once if it matters.

## Never
- Never describe a character's appearance in narration after their first scene.
